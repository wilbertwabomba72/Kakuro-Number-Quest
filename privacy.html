Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kakuro Number Quest handles information when you use the game. If you have questions, contact us at 508923216@qq.com.

Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Kakuro Number Quest does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Local Game Data

The game stores progress on your device, including completed levels, unlocked levels, star ratings, best times, current puzzle entries, music settings, and sound settings. This data is stored locally and is used to provide gameplay features.

Advertising and Device Identifiers

The game includes third-party advertising SDKs. With your permission where required by Apple App Tracking Transparency, advertising partners may use device identifiers such as IDFA, device information, ad interaction data, approximate location derived from network information, and diagnostic data to load, measure, limit, and improve ads.

Ad Configuration

The game may request advertising placement configuration from a backend service so that ads can be loaded and displayed at appropriate moments, such as after level completion. These requests may include basic device and app information needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

Third-Party Partners

Advertising and measurement SDKs may process data according to their own privacy policies. These partners may include advertising networks and mediation providers integrated into the app template.

Children's Privacy

The game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect app data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and third-party advertising partners are responsible for their own data handling practices.

Your Choices

You can manage tracking permission through iOS Settings. You can also reset or delete local game progress by removing the app from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

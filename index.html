About the Game

Kakuro Number Quest blends crossword-style clue reading with number logic. Choose a cell, enter a digit, and make every row and column add up to its clue.

The game includes 50 levels with gradually increasing difficulty, local progress saving, star ratings, hint support, background music, and sound effects.
